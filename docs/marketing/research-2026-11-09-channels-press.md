# Research: paid placements, app review sites, Québec/Canadian press, financial-literacy programs

Researched 2026-11-09 for the launch plan (`2026-11-launch-plan.md`). **[V]** = found on the outlet's own page or a primary document; **[P]** = partial / third-party; **[U]** = unverified or site blocked automated fetches (check by hand in a browser). "Price not published" = no public rate card.

## 1. Paid / sponsored blog and newsletter placements

### US/Canada parenting sites
- **Scary Mommy / Fatherly / Romper / The Dad (BDG)**: https://bdg.com/branded-parenting ; advertising@bdg.com. Branded content packages (article + video + social + display) for big brands; price not published, five figures+ **[P]**. Use for earned editorial only.
- **Motherly**: https://mother.ly/partner-with-us/ ; contact https://mother.ly/contact-us/ . Claims 40M moms/month; ~115k newsletter sold via Paved. Price not published **[V/P]**.
- **Today's Parent** (St. Joseph): https://www.todaysparent.com/partnerships/ (advertising, branded content, newsletter sponsorships, events). Price not published **[V, details blocked]**. Pitch editors for a feature (Québec dad, FR+EN) rather than buy.
- **SavvyMom**: https://www.savvymom.ca (advertise page 403) **[U]**; ask for the media kit.
- **Yummy Mummy Club / Ehm & Co Network**: sponsored editorial, giveaways, influencer network (claims 2M Canadian moms/month, 600+ creators). Price not published **[P]**. Good for influencer seeding.
- **Canadian Family**: dormant since 2014 **[P]**. **Mom Central Canada**: likely defunct **[U]**.
- **Montreal Families**: https://montrealfamilies.ca (403); archive ends Sept 2023; possibly inactive **[U]**.
- **ParentsCanada**: weekly newsletter on Paved; price not published **[P]**.
- **Family Fun Canada**: https://www.familyfuncanada.com/advertise/ sells newsletter inclusion or editor-written stand-alone emails; price not published **[V]**.
- **Macaroni Kid Canada**: https://canada.macaronikid.com/contact-advertising (hyper-local parent newsletters); price not published **[V]**.

## 2. App review / directory sites and listicles
- **Common Sense Media**: editor-chosen, never paid, no submission form; "Contact us" form and "Partnering with Us" page. Free **[V]**.
- **Educational App Store**: https://educationalappstore.com/developer ; submit https://www.educationalappstore.com/submit-product ; process https://www.educationalappstore.com/blog/educational-app-store-review-process . Free to submit, educator review (32-point framework), up to 10 working days, badge + review page; certification fee unclear, ask. Publishes "9 Best Chore Apps for Families and Kids in 2026" (Lucy Rome, Jan 2026) **[V]**.
- **AppAdvice**: unreachable; iOS-only; possibly a fee **[U]**. **Smart Apps for Kids**: dead **[V]**. **Teachers With Apps**: no review route **[V]**.
- **Product Hunt**: https://help.producthunt.com/en/collections/19793093-launch-guide ; schedule 1–30 days ahead; live 12:01 AM PT; category "Apps for kids"; recent chore apps got modest traction **[V]**.
- **AlternativeTo**: https://alternativeto.net/faq ; free; suggest as alternative to Greenlight/BusyKid/Homey/OurHome/S'moresUp; $5 priority review reported **[V/P]**.
- **Capterra / GetApp / Software Advice** (G2 Digital Markets): free basic profile; poor fit **[P]**.
- **BetaList**: https://betalist.com/faq ; paid (reported from $129), refundable if not selected **[V]**.
- **Indie Hackers**: community first, 2–4 weeks of presence before posting **[P]**.
- Listicles: NerdWallet https://www.nerdwallet.com/banking/learn/buzzy-banking-apps-for-kids-and-teens (bank-focused) **[U]**; Bankrate https://www.bankrate.com/personal-finance/best-money-apps-for-kids and https://www.bankrate.com/personal-finance/family-apps-to-manage-allowances-and-chores **[U bylines]**; Forbes Advisor "5 Best Allowance and Budgeting Apps for Kids" **[P]**; Fatherly 2018 (stale) **[V]**; Parents.com / Verywell / The Balance: no current roundup **[U]**. Pitch the "best free option" slot: 5 lines + press kit + store links to the byline and the generic editorial address.

## 4. Government / nonprofit kids' financial-literacy programs
- **FCAC Canadian Financial Literacy Database**: https://www.canada.ca/en/financial-consumer-agency/services/financial-literacy-database.html ; "temporarily unavailable for maintenance"; historically accepted resource submissions **[V]**. Watch for relaunch.
- **JA Canada**: https://jacanada.org/get-involved#partner-for-impact ; https://jacanada.org/about-us/contact-us ; partners are banks; 540k learning experiences 2025–26 **[V]**. Local-charter in-kind partnership plausible.
- **CPA Canada Financial Literacy Program**: https://cpa.cpacanada.ca/en/public-interest/financial-literacy ; 2,000+ free sessions/yr incl. parent/kids modules **[P]**. Offer the app as a free follow-up resource.
- **Desjardins Caisse scolaire**: https://www.caissescolaire.com/fr/parents ; own app; no external tools listed. Caisse-level "dons et commandites" form https://www.desjardins.com/ressources/pdf/f05-q30200-formulaire-dons-commandites.pdf **[V]**. Low odds.
- **Fondation Lucie et André Chagnon**: https://fondationchagnon.org/en/obtaining-funding/application-for-funding/ ; funds nonprofits; not a channel **[P]**.
- **Centres de services scolaires**: LIP art. 94; CSS policies bar commercial advertising in schools; Consumer Protection Act ss. 248–249 bans advertising to under-13s in Québec **[V]**. Do not market to kids or via schools; parent committees (FCPQ) and teachers only, parent-facing.
- **Vifa Magazine**: see §1; public-interest, free collaboration **[V]**.
